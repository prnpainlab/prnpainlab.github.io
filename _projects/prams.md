The Pain Recognition and Assessment Monitoring System (PRAMS) is an NSF-funded research initiative aimed at transforming how pain is recognized and managed in newborns and infants. Using advanced facial coding, physiological monitoring, and federated learning, PRAMS seeks to create a real-time, privacy-preserving system that continuously detects and assesses pain in neonatal patients.

**Funding:** National Science Foundation (Grant #2205472)
