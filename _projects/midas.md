MIDAS (Modeling Inter-individual Differences to Address Pain of Sickle Cell Disease) is a five-year study focused on adolescents and young adults living with sickle cell disease (SCD). The project collects whole-person health data including genetics, social determinants of health, and psychosocial variables to understand why pain experiences vary so widely across individuals.

**Funding:** National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NIH) — 1R01HL178882-01
